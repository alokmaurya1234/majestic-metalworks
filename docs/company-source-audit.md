# Company source audit

Research date: 2026-11-14. Primary source: [majesticmetalworks.com](https://majesticmetalworks.com/).

## Pages and route discovery

Five requested navigational pages were inspected: Home (`/`), About Us
(`/about/`), Services (the navigation's Product link, `/services/`), Clients
(`/client/`), and Contact (`/contact/`). Links and the WordPress page index also
revealed Contact Now (`/contact-now/`) and Appointment (`/appointment/`), both
inspected. The page index lists Customer Cabinet (`/customer-cabinet/`), which
appears account-oriented and was not opened. The page index also exposed default
WordPress posts, which are not company pages.

## Introduction and background

The site uses “Where Strength Meets Style” and describes Majestic Metal Works
as offering metal solutions, custom fabrication, welding, structural metalwork,
and architectural elements for residential and commercial settings. It refers
to “years of experience,” premium materials, advanced techniques, timely
delivery, and customer satisfaction. These are statements published by the
company website; no independent records were consulted to verify the claims.

The site's strapline reads “Manufacturer Of Wrought Iron Aluminum Gate Steel
Railing Gift Article.” This is transcribed as published and is unclear in
meaning, especially “Gift Article”; confirm the intended wording before reuse.

## Products and services published

The site shows eight product categories on Home and Services:

1. S.S Railing and Residential S.S. Railing.
2. Glass Railing.
3. Sliding Gate.
4. Automation Gate.
5. Window Glass.
6. Bathroom Glass.
7. Jhula.

The Home page also labels a product “Residence Cial Railing” (likely a site
spelling/content error). It is not silently corrected here; confirm intended
product naming with the owner.

Published Services details include automated sliding, swing, telescopic, and
boom barrier gates; remote or sensor access; and installation/after-sales
support. Sliding gates are described as manual or automated. Railing use cases
include balconies, staircases, terraces, entrances, and pools, with framed and
frameless glass options. Window glass types listed are clear, tinted, frosted,
laminated, and toughened. Bathroom glass mentions enclosures, partitions,
doors, mirrors, and frosted, clear, tinted, textured, or toughened finishes.
The company also describes Jhula/swing products.

These are published catalog/service descriptions, not independent evidence of
current availability, fabrication method, installation coverage, warranties, or
technical performance. The website makes rust resistance, durability, weather
resistance, sound/heat/UV control, corrosion resistance, and related performance
claims that need specifications or confirmation before being repeated as facts.

The Home page describes custom metal fabrication, welding, and structural
metalwork. No crane manufacturing was found in the inspected pages or page
index; do not include it in the redesign without owner-provided evidence.

## Existing clients

The Clients page displays these names: ITC, Reliance Group, NIT College
Jalandhar, IIT, and Nivia Sports. It describes the company as serving leading
real-estate names. These are website-published client associations and claims,
not independently confirmed relationships. The page did not supply usable
client logo files; do not create or substitute logos.

## Certifications and claims needing confirmation

- About Us displays an “ISO 9001:2015 Certified Company” badge. Obtain a valid
  certificate, issuing-body details, certificate number, and current validity
  before presenting certification as fact.
- Years of experience, quality, ethical business, adept team, distribution
  network, transport/logistics, reasonable prices, and timely delivery are
  published marketing claims. Request supporting or approved wording.
- Technical product performance and material claims need current specifications
  and product-level confirmation.
- Client relationships and project attribution need owner approval and ideally
  client permission for public logos or named case studies.

No independently verifiable registration, certification, facility, capacity,
project, or client records were available from this website review. The audit
does not treat site statements as independently verified facts.

## Asset findings

The WordPress media library exposes one usable official logo variant and eight
product-listing images. Three About-page images are architectural collages, not
clearly attributable project photographs. Small certification/badge images
are low resolution and their meaning/provenance is unclear. The site contains
no clearly identified workshop photography or usable client logos. Stock assets
collected separately are catalogued as illustrative only; they must never be
shown as Majestic Metalworks projects or facilities.

## Missing information and redesign recommendations

- Ask the owner for a current logo master/vector, authenticated workshop and
  completed-project photography, and approved project captions/locations.
- Obtain usable client logos and written permission before displaying them.
- Confirm exact legal/trading name, address, phone, email, service area, and
  preferred public contact details.
- Confirm which listed products/services are currently offered, including the
  meaning of “Residence Cial Railing” and “Gift Article.”
- Request evidence for ISO certification and substantiation for technical,
  experience, delivery, and quality claims.
- Gather product specifications, material grades/finishes, installation scope,
  warranties, maintenance guidance, and enquiry/quotation process if these are
  to be represented on the new site.
- Keep stock photography visually distinct and label it in internal content as
  illustrative. Prioritize authentic company imagery where available.
